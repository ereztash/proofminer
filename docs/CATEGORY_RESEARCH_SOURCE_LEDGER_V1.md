## Evidence discipline

Do not infer from these sources alone:

- that any competitor has strong retention;
- that public pricing equals realized willingness to pay;
- that vendor-stated outcomes are causal;
- that the category is large enough for venture-scale economics;
- that a user's long-term decision history creates switching value;
- that ProofMiner's proposed differentiation is defensible.

Those remain FIELD questions.
