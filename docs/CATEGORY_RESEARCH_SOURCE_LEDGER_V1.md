## Personal Decision Intelligence

### Decyde

- `https://decyde.me/`
- Public product currently advertises:
  - three free decisions;
  - individual + business use;
  - decision wizard;
  - specialized framework selection;
  - transparent scoring / verdict / next step;
  - Decision Profile built from previous decisions, priorities, goals, thinking style, risk tolerance, industry/role and history;
  - a daily surfaced-decision dashboard.
- This is direct evidence that `Personalized Decision Intelligence`, framework routing, decision profile and history are not defensible differentiation by themselves.
- Public page did not expose crawlable paid price figures in this check; do not infer a price.

### Decision Journal AI

- `https://decisionjournal.ai/`
- Current public flow:
  - conversational decision capture;
  - pre-outcome belief / intent;
  - one relevant bias flag;
  - outcome review approximately six weeks later;
  - calibration update.
- Public planned launch pricing at time checked:
  - Beginner $5/month;
  - Practitioner $20/month;
  - Expert/team $75/month.
- Useful as a price / retention reference for generic decision journaling, not as proof of willingness to pay realized at scale.

# Research implications used in v2

1. **Generic Personal Decision Intelligence is not a blank category.** Decyde directly occupies it.
2. **Decision memory alone is not differentiation.** Decyde, Aera and Cloverpop all publicly emphasize history / decision memory.
3. **Continuous enterprise retention depends on continuous external state.** Aera / Planview receive operational data and new decisions; ProofMiner cannot assume equivalent monitoring value without a signal channel.
4. **Professional positioning has project economics.** Insideflows publicly sells one-time strategic builds in the high-hundreds to several-thousand-dollar range and separates ongoing execution/support.
5. **Human professional judgment is a real substitute.** GrowthMentor / MentorCruise show recurring and episodic paid advice models.
6. **Therefore billing cadence should be inferred from observed decision density, not chosen because SaaS prefers monthly recurrence.**

---

# Evidence discipline

Do not infer from these sources alone:

- that any competitor has strong retention;
- that public prices equal realized WTP;
- that ProofMiner can command the same price;
- that transition episodes naturally last 60–180 days;
- that prior decision history improves later decisions;
- that authority / market-position decisions are frequent enough for subscription;
- that the category is venture-scale;
- that monitoring creates value without integrations;
- that ProofMiner's current differentiation is defensible.

Those remain FIELD questions.
