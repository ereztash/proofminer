# Strategic Wind Tunnel — Recursive Self-Run v1

Date: 2026-08-16

## Purpose

Run the newly defined Strategy Wind Tunnel on the current ProofMiner strategy itself.

This is a recursive governance test:

```text
build simulation governance
→ run current strategy through it
→ accept any strategy / tool changes it produces
→ rerun stop rule
```

The Wind Tunnel is not allowed to protect its own existence.

---

# 1. O_RECALLED

Help a professional at a consequential transition improve how they understand the problem and allocate scarce resources toward a desired professional market / opportunity state, while preserving decision authorship and learning from real outcomes.

Business telos: capture sustainable value without manufacturing engagement, decision volume or dependence.

---

1. architectural family: `Personal Decision Intelligence`;
2. first commercial domain: professional market position / professional transition;

# 3. EVIDENCE_PACKET

## F1 — observed external evidence

### Generic Personal Decision Intelligence already exists

Decyde publicly offers structured decision frameworks, scenario comparison, a personalized Decision Profile and decision history across career / business / money / life categories.

Source: https://decyde.me/

Decision implication: `Personal Decision Intelligence`, decision profiles, framework routing and history are not sufficient differentiation.

### High-price adjacent professional-positioning services exist

Insideflows publicly offers one-time Personal Brand OS packages at `$897`, `$2,697`, and `$4,497`, with higher-priced managed execution services.

Source: https://insideflows.com/

Decision implication: professional-position / authority work can support materially higher prices than generic decision-journal software, but vendor list prices do **not** validate ProofMiner willingness to pay.

### Direct authority / visibility engines exist

GetKnown publicly positions itself as an AI-powered reputation / visibility engine with audits, positioning, content visibility, PR and personalized roadmaps.

Source: https://getknown.com/platform/

Decision implication: "personalized authority roadmap" is not differentiation.

### Scenario stress testing is an established strategic method

OECD foresight guidance uses assumptions, scenarios and stress testing to test strategy robustness across plausible futures rather than predict one future.

Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/foresight-toolkit-for-resilient-public-policy_bcdd9304-en.html

Decision implication: a pre-FIELD wind tunnel has a defensible method family, but does not receive predictive authority.

### Prompt-only synthetic humans are poor substitutes for observed behavior

ACL 2026 research against 31,865 real shopping sessions reports only 11.86% action-generation accuracy for prompt-based LLM human-behavior simulation.

Source: https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.2034/

Decision implication: synthetic buyers cannot validate WTP / conversion / retention.

## F2 — structural evidence

- A buyer cannot demonstrate willingness to pay without an actual offer / commitment behavior.
- Longitudinal compounding cannot exist before a second materially related decision / outcome exists.
- Characterization-first cannot justify its extra effort if it produces the same decision / information request as a strong simple process.
- A 90-day episode cannot create recurring decision value for a participant who has only one consequential decision in the episode.
- A field simulation cannot validate a field-only claim by recursively simulating more stakeholders.

# 6. SCENARIO_RESULTS

## S0 — base case: consequential transition + multiple plausible moves

Conditions:

- user has a real market-position transition;
- several actions compete for scarce resources;
- user has meaningful professional expertise;
- external evidence is incomplete;
- more than one consequential decision is expected.

Result:

Candidate A remains plausible.

Characterization can expose endogenous criteria / process; external research can test market state; episode can contain multiple decisions.

**Status: SURVIVES.**

## S1 — user time / attention shock

Condition:

The user is willing to spend very little time reconstructing professional cases.

Result:

A rigid Characterization-First sequence becomes a liability.

Strategy must allow:

```text
direct clarification / strong simple
→ only escalate to case reconstruction if it can change the decision
```

**Change:** Characterization is a routed capability, not the mandatory first experience.

## S2 — signal-delay scenario

Condition:

Market outcomes take longer than the 90-day episode or are attribution-noisy.

Result:

The product cannot rely only on final business outcomes to learn.

It needs predeclared leading evidence / signposts while preserving attribution uncertainty.

**Status: CONDITIONAL.**

## S3 — competitor / substitute response

Condition:

General AI systems improve structured reasoning and decision memory; competitors copy framework selection / decision profiles.

Result:

Framework routing, memory and "personalized decision intelligence" commoditize further.

More robust differentiation must come from a composition competitors do not get from generic memory alone:

- live professional-transition boundary;
- case-grounded characterization when useful;
- current external field evidence;
- contestable resource allocation;
- actual execution / outcome lineage;
- transferability / stale-history rejection.

**Status: core strategy survives, category-language differentiation fails.**

## S4 — stakeholder veto: "I don't need to understand this; just do it for me"

Condition:

Target buyer values execution / implementation more than decision authorship.

Result:

ProofMiner may be solving the wrong paid job for that segment.

Potential outcomes:

- exclude segment;
- partner / hand off execution;
- evolve into concierge where judgment + execution is the product.

Cannot resolve by simulation.

**Status: FIELD_ONLY.**

## S5 — boundary shift

Condition:

The apparent "market-position" problem is actually an offer, product, operations, capability, or cash-flow problem.

Result:

Forcing the Professional Market Position ontology creates a category error.

Required strategy change:

`ProfessionalTransitionProject` remains top-level; `MarketPosition` is a specialization / route, not mandatory domain.

**Status: SURVIVES WITH BROADER PROJECT BOUNDARY.**

## S6 — characterization mechanism failure

Condition:

Professional self-application produces insight but the Strong-Simple arm already reaches the same material decision / research request.

Result:

Characterization does not earn its cost.

Required response:

collapse to Strong Simple for that class of case.

**Status: explicit kill condition retained.**

# 9. PRE-MORTEM

Assume ProofMiner fails commercially after the pilot stage.

## Failure 1 — clever characterization, no decision delta

Earliest sign:

Strong-Simple and Characterization-First repeatedly produce the same action / research request.

Response:

collapse characterization for those cases.

## Failure 2 — users pay for human judgment, not software mechanism

Earliest sign:

value drops sharply when human research / synthesis is removed.

Response:

accept concierge / adviser model or identify the narrow automatable primitive before claiming SaaS economics.

## Failure 3 — buyers want execution

Earliest sign:

real refusals cluster around "I need this done, not decided".

Response:

segment, partner, bundle, or change ICP rather than adding more analysis.

## Failure 4 — 90-day retention is artificial

Earliest sign:

few natural decision #2 events; sessions are mostly scheduled reviews with no allocation consequence.

Response:

move to episodic / reactivation billing.

## Failure 5 — synthetic simulation creates false confidence

Earliest sign:

internal stakeholder simulations converge while real users repeatedly surprise the model.

Response:

lower or eliminate synthetic layer; increase real evidence weighting; preserve FIELD_DEBT.

## Failure 6 — domain boundary is too narrow

Earliest sign:

many "market-position" cases resolve to offer / product / operations bottlenecks outside current model.

Response:

keep professional transition as top-level O; route to domain-specific lenses rather than force authority / positioning.

1. **The product should improve consequential professional decisions, not generic reflection.**
2. **Characterization is a routed source of endogenous evidence, not a mandatory ceremony.**
3. **External market evidence is required wherever the user cannot know the outside world from introspection.**

# 12. FIELD_DEBT

## FD1 — Characterization unique contribution

**Claim:** Characterization-First reveals a material distinction that Strong Simple misses often enough to justify cost.

Why load-bearing: central mechanism / differentiation candidate.

Why simulation insufficient: depends on real professional tacit knowledge and live decision context.

Smallest test: same suitable case, frozen baseline, Strong-Simple analysis first, Characterization only afterward without contaminating the first arm's record.

Decision changed by: unique decision / research / allocation distinction vs no material difference.

## FD2 — Willingness to pay at $1,500

**Claim:** suitable participant pays serious money after experiencing value.

Why load-bearing: commercial model.

Simulation limit: synthetic purchase intent is invalid.

Smallest test: exact real offer after intervention.

## FD3 — Decision density

**Claim:** a suitable transition generates multiple consequential decisions within an episode.

Why load-bearing: episode retention / business model.

Smallest test: follow real participant until decision #2 or episode end.

## FD4 — Execution behavior

**Claim:** user acts differently after the intervention.

Why load-bearing: representation / intention alone may not create value.

Smallest test: follow-up actual action / allocation.

## FD5 — Market response

**Claim:** revised action produces useful external signals / outcomes.

Why load-bearing: strategic value.

Smallest test: observe execution and predeclared signals; preserve attribution uncertainty.

## FD6 — Longitudinal compounding

**Claim:** decision #1 lineage materially improves decision #2 after transferability check.

Why load-bearing: long-term product retention / moat.

Smallest test: fresh-context shadow analysis vs longitudinal analysis at decision #2.

## FD7 — Paid job language

**Claim:** `professional transition / market position` maps to how buyers describe the problem.

Why load-bearing: acquisition / positioning.

Smallest test: record unprompted problem language from suitable participants before introducing category vocabulary.

## FD8 — Decision support vs execution substitute

**Claim:** enough target users pay for better decisions rather than primarily wanting execution.

Why load-bearing: product category / margins / delivery model.

Smallest test: real refusal / purchase reasons across suitable participants.
