# ProofMiner — Indispensability DOD v1

Date: 2026-08-16

## Governing ambition

> **Unskippable by value, skippable by design.**

The product must remain bypassable. It may not manufacture dependence through lock-in, dark patterns, streaks, artificial anxiety, forced waiting, notification pressure, engagement quotas or hidden switching costs.

The target is stronger:

> After experiencing the product on real consequential commitments, a suitable user voluntarily routes the next material commitment through ProofMiner because bypassing it creates a meaningful risk of losing a check, a pre-commitment record or accumulated learning they now consider decision-relevant.

---

# The product is DONE only if all five layers survive

## I1 — PRESENT AT THE MOMENT OF COMMITMENT

The user should not need to remember to "go use a decision app".

The product must be able to receive or detect a candidate commitment from the surfaces where commitments already form: calendar, tasks, proposals, projects, budgets, messages, documents or an explicit one-click handoff.

**Fail:** the primary behavior is opening a blank dashboard and reconstructing context manually.

## I2 — SELECTIVE, NOT CONSTANT

Most actions should pass without intervention.

The system intervenes only when support value plausibly exceeds support cost: material exposure, irreversibility, uncertainty, dependency, public commitment, delayed feedback or unusually weak evidence.

**Fail:** frequent friction teaches the user to bypass or disable the product.

## I3 — UNIQUE PRE-COMMITMENT VALUE

Before action, the product preserves a timestamped before-state that hindsight cannot recreate:

- intended action;
- resource exposure;
- candidate load-bearing assumption;
- relevant evidence / unknown;
- reversal condition;
- expected signal and horizon.

A generic later conversation cannot reconstruct this state with equal integrity.

## I4 — ACTION CONSEQUENCE

Every intervention ends in one of:

- `PASS` — proceed; the check found no justified reason to alter the commitment;
- `MODIFY` — sequence / scope / allocation / prerequisite changes;
- `PROBE` — a smaller discriminating observation precedes a materially larger commitment.

No credit for insight, surprise, eloquence, personalization or time-in-product without commitment consequence.

`PROBE` is forbidden when acting directly is cheaper, reversible and at least as informative.

## I5 — COMPOUNDING MEMORY

After the expected signal horizon, the product links real execution and outcome back to the before-state.

Over repeated commitments it may surface user-specific patterns only when source-linked and sufficiently supported, for example:

- recurrent planning error;
- recurring missing evidence type;
- repeated premature commitment class;
- situations in which prior instincts repeatedly survived challenge;
- reversal signals that proved useful or useless.

History remains contestable and may not become authority merely by age or repetition.

---

# Behavioral indispensability test

The product does not pass by survey enthusiasm alone.

A suitable user must demonstrate, across repeated consequential commitments, that they expect value from the check before outcome is known.

Strong evidence includes:

1. voluntary routing of a later material commitment through ProofMiner without researcher prompting;
2. low bypass when an intervention is offered and the user remains free to skip;
3. if ProofMiner is unavailable, the user delays the commitment or manually reconstructs part of the check;
4. willingness to pay to preserve the capability;
5. continued use is concentrated at consequential commitment moments rather than artificial daily engagement.

No single numeric threshold is canonical before FIELD establishes a sensible baseline.

---

# Removal test

After the user has completed enough real commitment cycles to understand the capability, temporarily remove ProofMiner from one suitable commitment surface.

Ask behaviorally, not rhetorically:

> **What does the user do when the safety layer is absent?**

Possible evidence of indispensability:

- recreates the check manually;
- deliberately postpones the commitment;
- requests the layer back;
- explicitly accepts the uncovered risk and proceeds.

**Fail:** does not notice the absence, proceeds identically, or substitutes a generic chat with no meaningful loss.

---

# Substitute kill test

Compare against the strongest cheap substitute available at the time, including a capable general AI conversation plus a simple decision template.

ProofMiner survives only if its advantage comes from at least one capability the substitute does not cheaply recreate:

- being present at the commitment surface;
- trusted pre-influence capture;
- cross-surface resource exposure;
- outcome-linked decision lineage;
- user-specific calibration accumulated from real prior commitments;
- selective triggering that reduces the burden of deciding when to seek support.

If a generic assistant plus a template repeatedly produces the same commitment outcome at similar effort, collapse the product toward that simpler mechanism.

---

# Anti-addiction invariant

The system may optimize **missed-support error** and **unnecessary-support burden**.

It may not optimize daily active use, session count, streak length or emotional dependence as proxies for product value.

A user who makes no consequential commitment this week may correctly have zero ProofMiner interactions.
